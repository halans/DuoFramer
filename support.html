Support

Answers to common questions. There's also Help inside the app, under Settings.

Getting started

Which devices does DuoFramer run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 27.2 or later. On iPhone Duo you also get frames shaped for its screens, the landscape editor on the inner screen, and tent mode.

How do I frame a photo?

Tap a template on the Templates tab, or the photo button, then choose a photo. DuoFramer fills in the camera, settings, date and place. Pinch and drag to adjust the crop, tap the sliders button to choose which details show, then tap Share to export.

Why can't I see all my photos?

DuoFramer probably has limited access to your library. Open the Settings app, tap Apps, then DuoFramer, then Photos, and choose Full Access. With limited access, only the photos you selected appear in DuoFramer's browser. You can always use the system photo picker instead.

Why don't my frames appear in DuoFramer's photo browser?

Frames DuoFramer saves to Photos go into a DuoFramer album and are hidden from its browser, so you don't frame a frame by accident. They're all in My Frames.

Frames

How does smart cropping work?

When a template crops your photo, DuoFramer finds the faces in it, or the most eye-catching part if there are none, and keeps them in view. It runs on your device. If you adjust the crop yourself, DuoFramer keeps your choice; Reset goes back to the automatic crop.

Can I frame several photos at once?

Yes. In the photo browser tap Select and choose up to 50 photos, or long-press a template and choose Use for Multiple Photos. They all get the same template and details, and you can swipe between them to adjust any one. Export All saves them to My Frames, and you can put the batch in a new collection.

Where do place names come from?

From the location stored in the photo. DuoFramer asks Apple Maps for the place name. You can show the place, the coordinates or both, or type your own in Details.

Can I add the weather?

Yes. In Details, choose the conditions and add a temperature, feels-like temperature, wind and humidity, in °C or °F. The weather is entered by hand.

iPhone Duo

What are iPhone Duo frames?

Frames at the exact shape of the Duo's screens: 2034 × 1398 pixels for the outer screen and 2670 × 1878 for the inner screen, in landscape. They fill the screen edge to edge when you play them back on your Duo.

I don't have an iPhone Duo. Can I still make Duo frames?

Yes. In Settings, turn on Show iPhone Duo Templates. They're shown automatically on a Duo and hidden elsewhere until you turn them on.

How does tent mode work?

Fold your iPhone Duo into a tent with DuoFramer open in landscape, and your frames play full screen on the outer display. Choose what plays with Show in Tent Mode on a collection, or in My Frames under Slideshow.

Sharing

How do I make a carousel?

Open a collection, tap More, then Create Carousel. Choose a size (4:5, 3:4, 1:1 or 1.91:1), a white or black background, a title and between 2 and 20 slides, then tap Create Slides and share them.

Is DuoFramer really free?

Yes. Every template and feature is included, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, ads or watermarks.
